Why the usage limit stopped looking red

Your usage plugin draws the quota bar with three ANSI colours: blue under 75 %, bright magenta from 75 %, and plain red from 90 %. Plain red is slot 1 of the terminal palette. I had lightened slot 1 to a rose (#ff6b81) so red text stays readable on black, but for a protan eye that rose turns into a light grey-tan, close to the greens and cyans, so the danger colour lost its darkness. A pure red (#ff3b45) looks like a dark olive instead, which is what stands out.
