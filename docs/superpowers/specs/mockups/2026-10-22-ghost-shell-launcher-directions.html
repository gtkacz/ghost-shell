GHOST-SHELL · PLAN 3A · LAUNCHER

Three launcher directions

Each one is live. Click a direction, then type: it searches the 50 apps on this machine, with your real kora icons, by name, generic name and keywords. Up/Down moves, Enter launches (simulated), Esc closes, and the brand block or Meta button reopens it. The power actions arm on the first press and act on the second, so a stray click can't shut the machine down. The recent files are made up. The bar is layout A at mockup scale, and the window behind it stands in for whatever you have open.

ICONS BAR KEY
A

DROP PANEL

The spec's version: a narrow panel dropping from the brand block. Empty, it shows pinned apps, recent files and every app; typing turns it into one result list. Power sits along the bottom.

B

TWO-PANE · KICKOFF-LIKE

Closest to Kickoff: categories on the left, apps in the middle, recent files and session actions on the right. Typing searches every category.

C

CONSOLE

A command prompt in the middle of the screen over a dimmed backdrop, more terminal than menu. Numbered results (Alt+1..9 launch directly); power and session are commands at the bottom. It doesn't drop from the brand block.
