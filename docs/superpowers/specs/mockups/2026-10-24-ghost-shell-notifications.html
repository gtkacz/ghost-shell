Plan 3b · notifications and the bell

Everything at mockup scale (1 px = 2 physical px), in the tokens of the reference mockup. Values are simulated; the app names, the drive and the INMET wording are this machine's. The toasts' stripes step at 15 fps and the text blinks in the boot cursor's four held steps, as the alert boxes already do.

1 · The bell, at the tray's end

The bell is the tray's last built-in icon, right before the view chip, as the reference mockup places it. The count sits on the bell as a small solid square: signal while the unread items are ordinary, crit while any unread critical item remains. Four states, left to right.

2 · Toasts, top-right under the bar

New notifications appear under the bar at the screen's right edge, newest nearest the bar, at most three at a time; the rest wait and the last line says how many. A bottom bar puts the stack above itself, growing upwards. Two densities to react to:

Normal toasts dismiss after the configured 6 s; the segmented line along the bottom is the time left, in held steps, and hovering holds it. Critical ones stay until dismissed and have no line. A boxed toast (warn or crit) is an AlertBox: stripes, arrival flicker, the cursor blink for its attention window, then held. The app's own icon follows the bar's icon style (mono here); a picture the sender attached replaces it at 40 px. Text scrambles in once; a replaced notification updates in place without scrambling again. The close cross and the chevrons are drawn, never glyphs.

3 · The centre, dropped from the bell

The same panel size as the launcher (392 × 560), hanging from the bell's right edge; with a bottom bar it rises from the bell instead. It takes keyboard focus on demand and closes when it loses it, on Esc, or after it opens something. Typing filters. Up/Down select, Enter opens, Delete dismisses the selected row.
